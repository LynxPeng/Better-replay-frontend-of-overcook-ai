# Art sources

## Current restaurant scene: user-supplied Unity edition

On2026-11-06 the user explicitly requested replacing the kitchen food, complete counters, furniture and decorations with the supplied Restaurant Bits package, plus solid side walls, wall art and lower counters. The current scene uses that local edition under `Assets/Resources/RestaurantBits` (455 art files:225 FBX,225 prefab,3 materials,2 PNG). Original `.meta` GUIDs are retained. No package scripts or demo scene are installed; local material copies are converted to URP. The original Unity package remains unchanged.

This edition is listed under the Unity Asset Store EULA, **not** the free edition's CC0 grant. The archive and extracted source art are excluded from Git. Rebuilds require the local art installation or the supplied archive; `scripts/build-viewer.ps1 -RestaurantPackage '<path>'` automatically invokes `scripts/import-restaurant-bits.py` with the project conda environment. The Windows player embeds its built resources and does not require Python or the archive to run. The local install script only accepts art extensions, rejects Windows/POSIX path traversal and confines all output to the resource directory.

Active art includes native counters/stoves, onion/tomato crates and individual ingredients, pot/plate/bowl/stew meshes, dining tables/chairs/tablecloths, refrigerator, sink, dish rack, jars, wall cabinets, solid walls, a doorway and occasional windows. The invented food wall reliefs have been removed. Native wall boards use their authored base/plane pivot and face into the room; decorated walls contain native kitchen furniture, not framed food artwork. The former custom top slab/lip are removed; counters and stove bases are fitted to0.60 Unity units. Fine chef clipping and character animation adjustment are explicitly deferred until the user changes the chef model.

## User-supplied sky and compact town exterior

Synty Simple Sky is imported locally into `Assets/SimpleSky` from the user's store account. Its original dome, sun/cloud meshes and `SimpleSky.png` atlas are used in a generated replay-background prefab with a dedicated URP shader. The shader fixes background depth writes off; clouds do not cast shadows. The frozen daytime sky follows camera translation while keeping world-space orientation, so pitching the camera does not rotate the horizon. Source: https://assetstore.u3d.cn/packages/3d/environments/assetstore-package-20001078 . This locally imported Store resource is excluded from Git; no CC0 claim applies.

`Cartoon Town – Low Poly Assets 1.0.unitypackage` is user-provided.438 art files are installed under ignored `Assets/Resources/CartoonTown`, preserving source GUIDs; 29 legacy C# scripts, old effects shaders, animations and demos are excluded. The scene uses two shops and two homes across the street beyond the rear windows and landscaped river gardens on both sides. Native assets include Water, Bridge_Beach, mixed/pink/yellow/light-blue flower patches, GrassStems/GrassStemsHigh, StonesX3/Stone_LowFlat_1, lemon/crowned/pine/simple trees, stone paths, fence plots, lamps, benches, flower boxes and boats. The continuous flat ground backing samples an upward-facing native grass triangle's atlas UV and leaves openings for the lower water surfaces; it uses no new image textures. Material atlases (`Colors`, `GlossyColors`, `Floor`) are preserved and adapted to URP. `build-viewer.ps1 -TownPackage '<path>'` can install the local package before checking its required assets. Original package source is not redistributed in Git.

## Generated tiles and retained free wood floor

The active kitchen/dining floor uses original, hand-authored procedural grey-greige tiles and bevel normals generated by `InteriorMaterialAssets`, with strong light/dark variation, slightly warm greige and pale bevel rims. The tile PNGs and metadata are committed in `Assets/Art/CafeSurfaces`. A locally generated orange-yellow counter palette derives from the licensed native atlas and is excluded from Git; its meta preserves the material GUID. Native slab and carcass corners are rounded in memory; a thin painted rim follows the worktop surface. Source FBX references remain in prefabs, and no derivative Store mesh is committed.

The former Original2K base-colour and OpenGL normal JPGs from Rob Tuytel's [Wood Planks Grey](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_grey) remain optional under `Assets/Art/WoodPlanksGrey`, together with source URLs, verified MD5 values, material and `.meta` files. Poly Haven's [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license) permits use and redistribution. Source pixels are unchanged.

RestaurantBits now additionally supplies the icecream machine, empty preparation tables, containers/flavours/cones, sundae bowls, varied completed meals, narrower fridge variants, a native doorway/door and a pizza oven. Decorative meal selection uses a local seeded shuffle outside the game grid.

## Earlier CC0 sources retained as fallbacks

All imported art committed under `OvercookedViewer/Assets/Resources/Models` comes from the following author-distributed CC0 packs. Original license files are retained beside each pack. Models are selected and fitted to the replay grid; materials are converted to URP, highlights reduced, foliage recolored, and some counters stretched vertically to match the station height.

| Pack | Author source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kenney Food Kit 2.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit | Onion, soup bowl, fallback pot/plate/tomato |
| Kenney Furniture Kit 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit | Fallback cabinets, tables, chairs and props |
| Kenney Nature Kit 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | Detailed trees, plants and flowers |
| Kenney City Kit Commercial 2.1 | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial | Distant street buildings and color atlas |
| KayKit Restaurant Bits 1.0, free edition | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Restaurant-Bits-1.0 | Textured counter, stove, pot, plate, tomato, dining furniture, windows and kitchen accessories |

The fallback KayKit files under `Models/KayKit` were obtained from the creator's public repository. Their original `LICENSE.txt` explicitly grants CC0 use. The OBJ/MTL models and `restaurantbits_texture.png` keep the creator's texture UVs; URP materials use the original gradient atlas. This grant does not label the separate current Unity Store installation as CC0.

The original user-provided `unity/Restaurant Bits for Unity v1.0.0 (18 Jul 2024).unitypackage` inventory contains225 FBX,225 prefabs,two PNGs,three materials,one demo scene and seven folder entries; no C# scripts. It is preserved locally and ignored along with the extracted installation. Source evidence for the Unity edition's license: https://marketplace.unity.com/packages/3d/props/food/kaykit-restaurant-bits-for-unity-282790 .

The closer kitchen-focused view retains a detailed upper streetscape: Bridge_Beach meshes connect the road across both rivers; native benches, flower boxes, plants and Clock dress the shopfronts. Water strips extend beyond normal framing. Three native Boat instances use project-authored looping translation/bobbing, opposite river directions, hidden bridge entries and off-screen exits; native foliage uses visible root-anchored sway. Ambient time is separate from replay pause, seek and speed. Day/night modes reuse SimpleSky's native day/night atlas columns and sun/moon/star geometry through property blocks, without changing source art. No source animations or legacy package scripts are imported.

The chef shapes, room/street assembly, palette materials, camera, animations, steam visualization and synthesized delivery chime are project-created. No models, textures or sounds from the commercial Overcooked games are included.
